# 3. Equipment Maintenance History

Three plant registers under Forms Hub → **Equipment maintenance and history**. They are parallel books, not one tree.

## 3.1 Feature list — the three cards

- **Power Plant Equipment History** — folders → equipment → four disciplines (3.2).
- **Sugar House Equipment History** — same pattern, with Location on the card (3.3).
- **Production House Equipment History** — flat list, no folder tree (3.4).

[Screenshot: 03-equipment-hub — Equipment maintenance and history, annotate: the three app cards]

### 3.1.1 How they relate

- Power and Sugar: **Equipment Specification**, **OEM Maintenance Schedule**, **Equipment Maintenance History**, per **Mechanical / Civil / Electrical / Instrument**.
- Production: specification and history only (no OEM schedule, no disciplines).
- Each plant has its own history HOD (chapter 8.6).
- **Maintenance Notes** is a separate module (chapter 4), not a fourth equipment card.

### 3.1.2 How to open

- **Home** → **Open Forms Hub**.
- Section **Equipment maintenance and history**.
- Click one of the three app cards (**Open**).

---

## 3.2 Power Plant Equipment History

### 3.2.1 What you see

- Header: **Power Plant Equipment History** — *Browse boiler, turbine and water treatment equipment by cards or tree.*
- Views: **Cards**, **Tree**, **Mind map**, **Starred**, **Move history**.
- Folder card: title, item count.
- Equipment card: name, **4 disciplines · Choose section**, **Tag:** when present.

[Screenshot: 03-power-explorer — Power hierarchy Cards view, annotate: folder vs equipment, Tag, Star]

### 3.2.2 Search

- Placeholder: **Search tag or name...**
- Tree opens matching branches.
- Move history search: **Search user or card...**

### 3.2.3 Open a machine

- Open the folder path.
- Click the equipment.
- Click a discipline: **Mechanical**, **Civil**, **Electrical**, **Instrument**.
- Detail shows Specification, OEM schedule, Maintenance History.

[Screenshot: 03-power-detail — Discipline detail, annotate: Specification, OEM schedule, Maintenance History]

### 3.2.4 Add a history record

- **Add Record** on **Equipment Maintenance History**.
- Fields: Season (Off-Season / Season), Year, Date of Start, Date of Finish, Outage / Observation, Action Taken, Maintenance Type (RM, PM, PdM, CBM, CM, RCM), Repair Cost (Rs.), Service (Internal / External), Provider, Responsible, Remarks, Before / After photos.
- If approval is on: save as draft, then **Submit all (N)** (or submit that row).
- **Download Excel** / **Download PDF** on the detail page.

### 3.2.5 Read history

- Columns: Entry ID, Season, Year, Start, Finish, Maint. Type, Observation, Action.
- Filters: **Search IDs, observations, actions...**, All Seasons / Off-Season / Season, All Years, Created Newest / Oldest first.
- Badges: **Pending**, **Needs modification**, **Approval pending**, **Delete pending**.

### 3.2.6 Star, Move, locks

- **Star this card** / **Remove from starred**.
- **Move** is same level only → **Move here**.
- Move history columns: User, Card, From, To, Date.
- Seeded / locked Power cards need **locked-card manage** for Power (chapter 8.1).
- Star / Move / Edit / Delete only when the tree is the live database tree `[TO CONFIRM]`.

### 3.2.7 Who can edit

- App assigned; not view-only.
- History delete: administrator only.
- Do not edit while **Approval pending**, except draft or **Needs modification**.

## 3.4 Production House Equipment History

### 3.4.1 What you see

- Header: **Production House Equipment History** — *All production house equipment cards — specs and maintenance history.*
- Flat grid. No Cards / Tree / Mind map / Starred / Move history.
- Card shows: name, house section, type, duty, capacity, spec count, history count.
- House sections: **Pan & Crystallizer**, **Evaporation**, **Clarification**, **Centrifugal & Drier House**.
- No OEM Maintenance Schedule.

[Screenshot: 03-production-list — Production House list, annotate: Search equipment, house section]

### 3.4.2 Search

- **Search equipment…** → **Search**.
- **Clear** removes the filter.

### 3.4.3 Add or update

- Open the card.
- **Equipment details:** Name, Type, Duty, Capacity → **Save details**.
- Imported: *Identity locked — ask an admin for locked-card manage access.*
- **Add Record** on history (same field set as Power/Sugar; observation is not forced).
- Submit for **Production House** HOD when approval is on.
- **Download PDF** (specification + history).
- **Delete card** removes the equipment — only if allowed.
