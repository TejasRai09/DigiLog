# 9. Profile and notifications

## 9.1 Profile (avatar)

- Click your avatar on the navbar.
- Shows name, email, **Department** (or *No department on file*).
- **Upload photo (PNG / JPEG)**.
- Crop: *Drag to center your face…* → **Save photo**.
- **Remove photo**.
- Other fields are read-only.

## 9.2 Notifications (bell)

- Panel title: **Notifications**.
- **Mark all read**.
- Empty: **No notifications**.
- Loading: **Loading…**.
- Per item: **Remove notification**.
- Times: **Just now**, **Nm ago**, and similar.
- Updates live, and every 60 seconds.
