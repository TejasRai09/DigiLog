# 4. Maintenance Notes

A short shared register of work the plant intends to do. It is **not** an equipment life-history row (chapter 3) and **not** a shift logbook (chapter 2).

## 4.1 Who can open it

- Navbar **Maintenance Notes**.
- Allowed: Sugar House, Power Plant, Production House staff.
- Engineering category uses **Sugar House**.
- Distillery category does **not** get this menu.
- Administrators see all plants.
- Unavailable copy: **Maintenance Notes is not available** — *This register is only for Sugar House, Power Plant, and Production employees.*

## 4.2 What you see

- Title: **Maintenance Notes**.
- Subtitle: *Shared register of work the plant intends to carry out. Anyone can add a note. Completed is set only after the plant HOD approves.*
- KPI tiles: **Pending**, **Awaiting HOD**, **Rework**, **Completed**.

## 4.3 Filters

- Search: **Search note, person, or activity**.
- Plant: **All plants**, or locked to **Sugar House** / **Power Plant** / **Production House**.
- Status: **All statuses**, **Pending**, **Approval Pending**, **Rework Required**, **Completed**.
- **Reset**.
- Empty: *No maintenance notes match these filters.*

## 4.4 Add a note

- Click **Add note** / **Add maintenance note**.
- **Plant*** — locked from your employee category, or chosen by admin.
  - Locked copy: *Plant is set from your employee category. Equipment comes from that plant’s hierarchy.*
  - Unlocked copy: *Plant decides which HOD can close the note. Equipment comes from that plant’s hierarchy.*
- Tick **Not related to a specific equipment**, or pick **Equipment***.
- **Activity type***:
  - Inspection
  - Repair
  - Replacement
  - Lubrication
  - Cleaning / Descaling
  - Overhaul
  - Calibration
  - Painting / Civil
  - Other
- **Activity (short text)*** — max **80** characters.
- **Details (optional)**.
- **Photos** (optional, max as on form).
- **Documents** (optional) — PDF or Word, 10 MB each.
- **Submit** / **Save changes** / **Cancel**.

## 4.5 Submit for HOD

- Row actions: **View**, **Edit**, **Submit** (when allowed).
- **Submit for completion** modal: **Completion remarks (optional)** → **Submit for approval**.
- Status becomes **Approval Pending**.
- HOD works in **Approvals** → **Maintenance Notes** (chapter 5.3).

## 4.6 View only

- *You have view-only access. You can read the register but cannot add or submit notes.*

## 4.7 Statuses (feature list)

- **Pending** — written, not yet sent to HOD.
- **Approval Pending** — waiting for HOD.
- **Rework Required** — HOD sent it back.
- **Completed** — HOD approved.
