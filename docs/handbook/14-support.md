# 14. Support and escalation

## 14.1 Who to call

- **Password / Microsoft / Google** — IT administrator. No self-service reset.
- **Missing card, form, dashboard, upload tab** — DigiLog administrator (chapter 8.1).
- **View-only banner** — administrator, Forms Hub access.
- **Waiting too long for approval** — your HOD (chapter 5).
- **Wrong plant on Notes** — administrator, employee category.
- **Locked equipment card** — administrator with locked-card grant.
- **Staging vs production** — administrator. Staging is testing only.
- **Dashboard number looks wrong** — data owner or the person who fills the source form. Do not edit the dashboard.
- Named phone and email: `[TO CONFIRM]`.
