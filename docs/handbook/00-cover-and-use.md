# 0. Cover and how to use this handbook

## 0.1 Cover

- **Title:** DigiLog Operator Handbook and Feature List

- **Owner:** Plant training / DigiLog administrator `[TO CONFIRM]`

## 0.2 What this book is

- A **handbook:** how to sign in, fill a form, approve a row, upload a file, read a dashboard.
- A **feature list:** every Forms Hub app, every form card, every Approvals tab, every Upload slot, every Config section.
- Screenshot boxes are empty until photos are dropped in `docs/handbook/images/`.

## 0.3 How to read the numbers

- **1** = chapter (module).
- **1.1** = screen or topic.
- **1.1.1** = form card, tab, or sub-step.
- Under each number, points are **bullets**.
- Button names match the screen, in **bold**.

## 0.4 Who should read which chapter

- **New user:** 1 Getting started, then your module.
- **Operator / chemist / sampler:** 2 Operation Form, 10 Workflows.
- **Maintenance:** 3 Equipment, 4 Notes.
- **HOD:** 5 Approvals, plus 2 and 3.
- **Data owner:** 6 Data Upload, 7 Dashboards.
- **Administrator:** 8 Config, 1 Getting started.
- **Management:** 7 Dashboards.

## 0.5 Notes, tips, cautions

- **Note.** Extra fact. Does not stop the job.
- **Tip.** Faster way.
- **Caution.** Wrong action can lose data or send a bad figure to a dashboard.
